What to say

  • Activity first, then travel only when the location changes.
  • Activities and trips have separate durations.
  • Shared trips are copied to companions and linked by a shared-trip ID; the destination activity is never copied.
  • The prototype is local: no server submission, no continuous GPS, no live geocoder, no verified household access.
  • Completion here is a local export, not an institutional submission.
Illustrative values only. Fictional example records are teaching material, not survey observations or calibration evidence. Optional map links open OpenStreetMap and need internet. No continuous GPS tracking, server submission, geocoding, verified sampling frame or city-boundary validation is implemented.
